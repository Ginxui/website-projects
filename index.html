Sigil & Words

The Dondarrion banner is a field of deep purple torn through by a single forked bolt of black lightning — a strike frozen mid-fall, sudden and absolute. Heralds of the Stormlands say the purple stands for the storm-dark sky, and the lightning for a house that moves before its enemies expect it.

Their words, “Swift and Sure,” are less a boast than a method: Dondarrion knights favor speed and certainty over numbers, striking hard and vanishing before the thunder catches up to the flash.

No house's arms have felt more literal in the last hundred years. During the War of the Five Kings, that lightning bolt stopped being only a sigil.

History

Ancient Days

First Men of the Stormlands

Unlike many storm lords who trace their line to Andal conquerors or to the old Storm Kings of House Durrandon, House Dondarrion claims descent from the First Men who held these hills and marshes long before the Andals ever raised sail. Blackhaven predates most of its neighbors, a dark keep weathering centuries of storms rolling in off Shipbreaker Bay and down from the Dornish Marches.

Long Peace

Wardens of the Marches

For generations, Dondarrion lords served as a quiet buffer between the Stormlands and Dorne, their small keep never as wealthy as Storm's End nor as famous as the great seats of the realm — respected rather than renowned, which suited them.

War of the Five Kings

The Lightning Lord Rides

That changed with Lord Beric Dondarrion. Sent by Eddard Stark to bring the King's justice to the Mountain, Gregor Clegane, Beric was cut down and left for dead — and returned. Again, and again, kept from death's door by the fire priest Thoros of Myr, he rode on as the Lightning Lord, and the purple-and-black banner became the mark of the Brotherhood without Banners, a band that answered to no king and rode for the smallfolk the war had forgotten.

Legacy

A Name Larger Than Its Seat

House Dondarrion remains a minor house by lands and gold, but its name now carries further than Blackhaven's walls ever could — carried, fittingly, on the same lightning that marks its banner.

Lord Beric Dondarrion

The Lightning Lord

Once a promising young lord and a favorite at tourneys, Beric Dondarrion was dispatched by Lord Eddard Stark to arrest Ser Gregor Clegane at the outset of the War of the Five Kings. Cut off, ambushed, and killed in the field, he was brought back by Thoros of Myr's prayers to the Lord of Light — the first of several deaths he would shrug off in the years that followed.

As leader of the Brotherhood without Banners, Beric turned a scattered band of leaderless soldiers into a guerrilla force that struck lords and smallfolk- preying outlaws alike, all under the purple-and-lightning banner of his house. Each resurrection cost him more of himself, yet he rode on, swift and sure, until the fire in him finally passed to another.

Blackhaven

Blackhaven sits in the Stormlands near the Dornish Marches, a dark, weathered castle built to watch the passes rather than to impress. It has never held the wealth of Storm's End or the size of Nightsong, but its position has made its lords into border-wardens by necessity.

Storms roll through often here, and locals swear the lightning strikes the old keep more than any other hold in the region — a coincidence the Dondarrions have never much minded, and a smallfolk legend they've never much denied.
